Science Exhibition/Poster Presentation – Rules and Regulations

Theme:

  • Climate Change
  • Man on Mars
  • Sustainable Life
  • Eco-Friendly Technology
  • Latest Technology and Advancement

1. General Guidelines

  1. The exhibition is open to students of all grades as specified by the organizing committee.
  2. Participant may take part as a team (Science projects maximum of 3 members per team). (For poster presentation team size maximum 2 members.)
  3. Every project/poster must be original and prepared by the participants themselves.
  4. All exhibits must be approved by the science teacher or coordinator before submission.

2. Project Display and Presentation:

  1. Each exhibit should include:
    • A model or demonstration,
    • A display chart/poster, and
    • A brief written report (150–300 words) explaining the concept, aim, materials used, procedure, and conclusion.
  2. The project/poster must be labeled clearly with the title, participant names, class, and school name. Students must bring decorative items/table cloth if they want to decorate their table.
  3. Models can be working or non-working.
  4. Each team will be given a table space (approx. 3 ft × 3 ft) and access to electricity (if requested in advance).
  5. Dangerous chemicals, explosives, open flames, or sharp tools are not allowed.

3. Time and Conduct

  1. Participants must set up their exhibits one hour before the exhibition begins.
  2. Each participant/team will have 3–5 minutes to explain their project/poster to the judges.
  3. Participants should remain at their stalls throughout the exhibition to answer visitors' questions.
  4. Proper decorum and discipline must be maintained at all times.

4. Judging Criteria - Science Exhibition

Projects will be evaluated on the following basis:

Criteria Marks
Scientific thought & creativity 20
Originality & innovation 15
Practical application / usefulness 20
Presentation & clarity 15
Working model / demonstration 15
Overall impression 15
Total 100 marks

5. Awards and Recognition

  1. Certificates will be given to all participants.
  2. Prizes will be awarded for the Best Exhibit, Most Innovative Idea, and Best Presentation in each category.
  3. The judges' decision will be final and binding.

6. Safety and Discipline:

  1. Participants must ensure safety in handling all materials and equipment.
  2. Any project violating safety rules or school norms will be disqualified.
  3. The organizing committee is not responsible for any loss or damage to exhibits.

7. Poster Specifications

  1. Size: A3 / A2 / chart paper (as decided).
  2. Medium: Hand-drawn posters using paints, crayons, sketch pens, or colored pencils.
  3. Each poster must be an original creation of the participant(s).
  4. Participants must bring the prepared posters with them.

8. Judging Criteria - Poster Presentation

Posters will be evaluated based on:

Criteria Percentage
Creativity and originality 30%
Relevance to the theme 25%
Visual impact and presentation 20%
Neatness and clarity 15%
Message or slogan effectiveness 10%
